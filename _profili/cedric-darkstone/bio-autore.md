## Bio breve (~45 parole, per Author Central e social)
Cedric Darkstone writes practical guides for people who would rather do the thing than
read about it. He turns intimidating hobbies into simple, repeatable systems, with
templates and checklists you can use tonight. Confidence comes from playing, not from
one more chapter.

## Bio lunga (~140 parole, per Author Central)
Cedric Darkstone writes hands-on guides for beginners at the tabletop and beyond. His
approach never changes: cut the filler, hand you a repeatable system, and give you the
templates and checklists to use it tonight. He writes for the nervous first-timer, the
player about to run their first game, the hobbyist staring at a rulebook the size of a
phone book, and gets them doing fast, because confidence is built by playing, not by
reading one more page. Every book ends with something you can put on the table right
away. When he isn't writing, he's at a table somewhere, rolling dice and taking notes on
what actually works. Follow Cedric on Amazon to hear when the next guide lands.

brand si regge sul metodo, non su titoli falsi. Se Cedric ha una storia vera da
raccontare, si aggiunge.
