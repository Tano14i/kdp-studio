## Appendix: Rolls and Seeds

This appendix is fuel. When you need a name, a rumor, a reward, or a whole next adventure
and you need it now, roll here. Everything is built to be used live, mid-session, without
breaking the flow. Tear these pages out or copy them from the download pack and keep them
under your screen.

### d20 rumors for any tavern

Roll two or three when the party listens around a town. Some are true, some are bent, and
that mix is what makes a place feel alive.

```
 1  A merchant went out on the old road and only his cart came back.
 2  The miller pays too well for grain nobody can eat.
 3  Lights move in the burned watchtower on moonless nights.
 4  The priest has not opened the chapel crypt in a year.
 5  A child in the next village can name the day a stranger will die.
 6  The lord's new advisor casts no reflection, they say.
 7  Wolves this winter hunt in a line, like soldiers.
 8  Someone is buying old maps of the drowned quarter, no questions.
 9  The well went sweet the same week the healer went missing.
10  A gambler won three nights straight, then vanished owing nothing.
11  The bridge toll doubled and the toll-keeper is new and pale.
12  Farmers found their scarecrows moved closer to the house each dawn.
13  The mine reopened, but no ore has come out and no miner has come up.
14  A traveling troupe left one wagon behind, locked, and did not return.
15  The baker's bread keeps the sick alive longer than it should.
16  Two moons were seen last week. Nobody agrees which night.
17  The graves on the hill face the wrong way now.
18  A debt-collector arrived asking for a family that died out generations ago.
19  The river brought down a door, carved, from no house anyone knows.
20  Everyone in the almshouse had the same dream, and drew the same tower.
```

### d20 rewards beyond coins

Treasure the players will remember does something or points somewhere. Roll when they win.

```
 1  A ring that warms when a lie is spoken nearby.
 2  A map with one room marked that is not on any other map.
 3  A favor owed by someone powerful, in writing, signed.
 4  A key with no lock yet found, humming faintly.
 5  A vial that turns any water to clean drinking water, once a day.
 6  A cloak that muffles sound around its wearer.
 7  A ledger of debts, one of which is the villain's.
 8  A whistle only certain animals answer.
 9  A coin that always lands on the same face.
10  A letter of introduction to a guild or a court.
11  A lantern that burns with no fuel but only shows the truth.
12  A dagger that never needs sharpening and never draws blood it should not.
13  A deed to a ruined property with a secret still inside.
14  A songbook whose last page is torn out and coveted.
15  A pair of boots that leave no tracks.
16  A jar of soil that keeps one plant alive anywhere.
17  A mask that lets the wearer pass for someone they have seen.
18  A compass that points at what the holder most fears.
19  A seal that opens one specific door, somewhere.
20  A name. Only a name, whispered by the dying, that matters later.
```

### Six adventure seeds, ready to grow

Each seed is a whole adventure in the shape from Chapter 9: a goal, a three-clue path, and
a clock. Pick one, fill the blanks with your table's names, and run the one-hour prep on
it. Together with the starter adventure, these give you a season of play.

```
SEED 1 — The Salt in the Well
   Goal: find why the town's water is poisoning people
   Clues: a dead fox upstream · a bought-off inspector · a rival town's map
   Clock: the harvest festival is in three days and the whole town will drink

SEED 2 — The Debt of Ash
   Goal: protect a family from a collector who is not human
   Clues: a contract signed in a dead grandparent's hand · a witness who fled ·
          a symbol burned in the barn
   Clock: the collector returns at the next new moon to take "what is owed"

SEED 3 — The Quiet Mine
   Goal: learn why the reopened mine sends no one back up
   Clues: a miner's boot at the entrance · a cut rope · a fresh wall where none belongs
   Clock: the mine owner hires more workers tomorrow, sending them to their deaths

SEED 4 — The Wrong Heir
   Goal: settle who truly inherits a manor before a feud turns to blood
   Clues: a forged will · a servant who was there · a portrait painted over
   Clock: the reading of the will is at dusk, and armed men are already gathering

SEED 5 — The Troupe's Last Wagon
   Goal: open the locked wagon a vanished troupe left behind
   Clues: a playbill for a show that never happened · a key swallowed by a fish ·
          a child who remembers the songs
   Clock: whatever is inside is waking, and it wants an audience

SEED 6 — The Turned Graves
   Goal: stop the dead on the hill from rising, now facing the town
   Clues: a grave-digger's confession · a stone with a scratched-out name ·
          a priest who left in a hurry
   Clock: the graves turn a little more each night; on the seventh they open
```

Between the starter adventure and these six seeds, you will not run dry for a long time.
Roll, fill, prep, and play. The table is waiting.
