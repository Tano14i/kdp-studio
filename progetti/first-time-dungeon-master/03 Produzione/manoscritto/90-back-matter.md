## Quick-Reference: The Table Screen

Tear these pages out, or copy them from the download pack, and keep them where you sit.
Everything here is meant for use during play, not prep. When the game is live and your mind
goes blank, this is the page you look at.

```
MAKE A RULING (Chapter 4)
   1 What are they really trying to do?  2 Which ability fits?
   3 Stakes out loud (success / failure)  4 Roll, narrate, move on
   Difficulty fast: Easy 10 · Medium 15 · Hard 20 (unsure? 15)

IMPROV FIRST MOVES (Chapter 5)
   Repeat it back, then "yes, and" / "yes, but". Flat no only for the impossible.

WHO IS THIS NPC? — roll d20 (Chapter 5, Table A)
   1 nervous clerk  2 talkative veteran  3 widow running the trade
   4 official wanting a bribe  5 cheerful liar  6 youth in over their head
   7 blunt laborer  8 odd peddler  9 drunk who saw something real
  10 sure believer  11 grieving parent  12 old rival  13 gossip for coin
  14 guard who hates the job  15 healer low on supplies  16 obsessed collector
  17 reformed criminal  18 stern elder  19 charmer with an angle  20 fearful outsider

WHAT COMPLICATES THIS? — roll d20 (Chapter 5, Table B)
   1 watcher in shadow  2 rival group  3 true but incomplete  4 a painful price
   5 wrong person overhears  6 less time than they thought  7 old enemy involved
   8 a lie unravels  9 terrain turns  10 contact switched sides  11 reinforcements
  12 target has fled  13 trap for someone else  14 a debt comes due  15 goals conflict
  16 witness wants something  17 the way is guarded  18 someone is recognized
  19 reward isn't as promised  20 a sound draws attention

ENCOUNTER BUDGET (Chapter 7)
   Standard = one threat point per player. Easy = half. Hard = players x1.5.
   One point ≈ a monster at the party's level. Mix sizes. Turn a danger lever:
   terrain / reinforcements / an exit.

FIVE-ROOM LOCATION (Chapter 8)
   1 entrance+guardian  2 puzzle/roleplay  3 setback  4 climax  5 reward+twist

KEEP IT MOVING (Chapter 9)
   Plot facts are found automatically. Three clues to every conclusion.
   When they stall, start a clock. End the night on a question.

INSTANT NAMES

   Grud · Nix · Halla · Vane · Sump · Castellan
```
